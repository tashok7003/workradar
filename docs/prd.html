WorkRadar: Product Requirements

One AI-assisted view of a team's work, so a manager knows what needs them without opening four tools.
Ashok · v1.0 · October 2026
Status: MVP built on mock data

01Problem statement

An engineering manager's team works across Jira, GitHub, Slack and email. Each tool shows its own slice and its own idea of priority, and none of them answers the manager's real morning questions: what is the top work today, what happened, what needs me now, and who is blocked? Managers rebuild that picture by hand, tab by tab, several times a day. The costliest gap is work that is blocked on the manager (a decision, a sign-off, a reply). It sits in a DM or an email thread, invisible in every tracker, and usually surfaces only at the next stand-up, a day late.

02Target users

  • Primary: engineering managers and team leads with 5–12 reports whose work spans a tracker, a code host, chat and email.
  • Secondary: tech leads who triage for their team, and product managers who track delivery across the same tools.
  • Later: directors who need a cross-team risk view (not in MVP).

03Key pain points

  • Tool-hopping: four tools and dozens of tabs to answer one question.
  • Invisible blockers on the manager: asks live in DMs, not in any status field.
  • Conflicting priority: a P2 ticket, a CTO email and an on-call ping use different scales.
  • No "what happened today": merges, deploys and closes are scattered across feeds.
  • Search is per-tool: one customer issue spans a ticket, a PR, a thread and an email.
  • Noise: FYIs look the same as asks that need action.

07MVP scope

In: one team; Jira, GitHub, Slack and email as mock data; dashboard; AI briefing with item citations and a rules-based fallback; priority feed with an explained score; item detail; cross-source search; Assign, Mark done and Snooze with Undo; source-sync, AI-failure and empty states.

Out: real OAuth integrations and write-back to source tools; notifications and digests; multiple teams; user-tuned scoring; native mobile apps.

Design principle: rules rank, AI explains. Ranking is deterministic so the order is stable and every point is visible; Claude only summarises and drafts, and every item it cites must exist in the data.

08Assumptions

  • Managers will check one dashboard daily if it saves them the tool-hop.
  • Source data carries enough structure (status, owner, due date, severity, links) to rank reliably.
  • "Blocked on the manager" can be detected from blocked statuses plus direct asks; the MVP's mock data marks it explicitly.
  • One identity per person can be mapped across tools.
  • Read-only OAuth scopes for these tools are acceptable to IT and security.
  • Managers trust a transparent rule-based order more than an AI-chosen one.
  • One cached briefing call per page load keeps model cost low.
